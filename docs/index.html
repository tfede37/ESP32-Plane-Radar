📡 ESP32 Plane Radar + Weather

Flash the firmware to your Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.28 — right from this page.

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop (they support Web Serial).
  2. Plug the board into your computer with a USB-C cable.
  3. Click Connect below and choose the serial port.
  4. Click Install and wait — it takes about a minute.
Your browser doesn't support Web Serial — please use desktop Chrome or Edge. This page must be served over HTTPS to flash.
If the board isn't detected: put it in download mode — hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT — then click Connect again.

After flashing: the device creates a PlaneRadar-Setup Wi-Fi hotspot. Join it, open http://192.168.4.1 (or http://plane-radar.local), then enter your home Wi-Fi and your latitude / longitude. Full details are in the README.

Your Wi-Fi credentials and location are stored only on the device, never in this firmware.
